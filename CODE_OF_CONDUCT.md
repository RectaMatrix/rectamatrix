# Code of conduct

RectaMatrix is committed to a respectful, constructive, and technically open
community.

Participants are expected to communicate with empathy, critique ideas rather
than people, respect different experience levels, and accept moderation. Abuse,
harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberately disruptive
behavior are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Reports should be
made privately through the repository's security-advisory channel when public
discussion would expose personal or sensitive information. Maintainers will
protect reporter privacy as far as reasonably possible and apply this policy
fairly and proportionately.
