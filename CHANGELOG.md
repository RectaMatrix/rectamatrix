## [0.1.0-preview.1] - 2026-07-26

### Added

- Format Candidate 1 specification in normative English and informative German.
- Deterministic TypeScript encoder and decoder for binary and UTF-8 payloads.
- Seven symbol sizes from 24×16 to 144×96 modules and four ECC levels.
- Fixed-header whitening, RM-LZ1 compression, CRC-32C, and Reed–Solomon coding.
- Image detector, browser camera scanner, and SVG, PNG, JPEG, and ZPL output.
- Standard and Compact Quiet Zone profiles.
- 95 canonical encoder, decoder, negative, and image conformance artifacts.
- Interactive browser demo and focused API examples.
